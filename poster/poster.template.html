1Can you trust what an LLM reads?

Not its confidence. Check every answer with code.

Sanitizer: injection patterns in EN/KO/JA/ZH, field whitelist Arithmetic: subtotal + tax + tip + discount = total Date plausibility: a real date in the receipts' era Two-reader: the total and date must appear in the OCR text
