Edge labels = share of the 300 SROIE scans that took that path (run of 2026-10-31). Model self-confidence is never used to approve.

Reading the numbers: in lane A, 43.7% are accepted at T1, 51.7% at T3, 0.3% at T4, 4.3% go to people. Code checks caught 5 of Flash-Lite's 8 wrong answers and 38 of the local model's 51. The oracle (knows the answers) would send only 27% to the cloud for 98.7%: the escalation signal, not the model, is the bottleneck.
